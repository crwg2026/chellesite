Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Untwine Atelier ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit untwinehair.com, book through our Booksy page, or receive our mobile braid takedown services, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or booking a service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Contact and booking details — your name, phone number, email address, and service address — when you book an appointment or contact us.
  • Appointment details, such as the service booked, your hair's condition, and notes you share to help us prepare.
  • Photos or videos you send us of your hair for assessment or booking purposes.
  • For children's appointments (Kids Braid Takedown, ages 3–12), booking information is provided by the child's parent or guardian.

Information collected through our booking partner: Bookings and payments are processed through Booksy. Information you enter there — including your card details and deposit payments — is collected and processed by Booksy under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. Cash is not accepted for our services.

Information collected automatically: Our website may collect basic technical information (such as browser type, device, and pages visited) through cookies or similar tools, used to operate and improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and provide your appointments, including reminders and pre-visit instructions.
  • Travel to and perform services at your location.
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • Send occasional updates or promotions about Untwine Atelier — only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time.
  • Improve our services and website.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run our business:

  • Booksy and payment processors, to process bookings, deposits, and payments.
  • Service providers that help us communicate with you (for example, messaging or email tools).
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, clients, or business.

4. Photos and Social Media

With your permission, we may photograph or record your hair before, during, or after your service for our portfolio and social media accounts (including TikTok, Instagram @untwine_atelier, and our Google Business Profile). For children's services, this is done only with the permission of the parent or guardian who booked the appointment. You may decline, or ask us to remove posted content, at any time — contact us and we will take it down.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep booking and contact information only as long as needed to provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request a correction or deletion of your information.
  • Opt out of promotional messages (reply STOP to texts, or contact us directly).

We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services for children (ages 3–12) are booked by a parent or guardian, whose information we collect for the booking — not the child's. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website links to third-party sites and services (such as Booksy and Instagram). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
