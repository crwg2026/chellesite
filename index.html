About the Experience

We've made takedown
an act of self-care.

Untwine Atelier was born from a simple truth: the takedown process is one of the most important — and most overlooked — parts of your hair journey. Done carelessly, it can cause breakage, tangling, and damage that sets your growth back significantly.

We believe your natural hair deserves expert, attentive hands at every stage — especially before your next appointment. Our specialized services are slow, intentional, and deeply caring.

Our Story

A Service Born From
Passion & Purpose

Untwine Atelier was founded on one foundational belief: every woman deserves to have her hair handled with expertise, intention, and love — especially during the vulnerable process of takedown.

Too many women have experienced the frustration of sitting for hours trying to undo their braids at home — only to end up with breakage, matting, and a scalp that's been through too much stress. We saw a gap in the market for a service that treated takedown not as an afterthought, but as a premium, specialized experience.

Our specialist brings professional training, a gentle touch, and a genuine passion for natural hair wellness to every single appointment. When you sit in our chair, you're in the hands of someone who truly cares.
